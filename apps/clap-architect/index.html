Clap Architect

Clap a rhythm, hear it as drums

Turn Audio on and allow the mic. Tap the big pad (or Record): after a 4-click count-in, clap a rhythm for two bars — each clap lands on the nearest of the 8 steps above.

The loop then plays your pattern as drums (kick, snare, hat by step). Tap step numbers to edit by hand; ▶ stops and restarts. Clap detection is only on while recording, so the drums cannot retrigger it.
