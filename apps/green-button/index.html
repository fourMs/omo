Green Button

Green button swell

Turn Audio on, then hold the green circle — drag your finger inside for more vibrato. Release anywhere to fade out.

Where you touch shapes the sound. Near the centre it is a soft, round tone; towards the edge it grows brighter, wider and more resonant. Around the circle the colour changes: the top is brassy, the bottom hollow, the left dark and the right nasal. Each hold starts soft and blooms into its bright colour over about a second.
