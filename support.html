What this browser exposes — useful before a workshop.

    Tap Test motion on iOS — permission only unlocks after a gesture.

    Motion sensors on iPhone

    1. Open the app in Safari itself — not inside Instagram, Messenger, or an e-mail app (use Open in Safari from the share sheet).
    2. Tap the play surface once. Safari asks to access Motion and Orientation.
    3. Tap Allow, then hold the phone still while the app calibrates.

    No dialog, or tapped Cancel by mistake? Reload the page and tap again — Safari only re-asks after a reload. If that fails, close the tab and open the link fresh. Lockdown Mode blocks motion sensors entirely.
